Privacy Policy.

Last updated November 16, 2026.

This policy explains what information we collect through this website, why we collect it, and the choices you have. "We", "us" and "our" mean Gays Against Groomers.

Information you give us

  • Contact form. Your name, email address, the topic you choose and your message.
  • Speaking invitation form. Your name, email address, phone number if you provide it, your organization, and the details of your event, including your speaking fee budget and whether travel is covered.
  • Sponsorship inquiry form. Your name, email address, your company or organization, its website and your budget if you provide them, and the details of what you would like to promote.

We use this information only to read and respond to your message, invitation or inquiry. Form submissions are delivered to us by Formspree, a form-processing service, under the Formspree Privacy Policy.

Newsletter

When you sign up for our newsletter, we collect your email address and use it only to send you our newsletter. Signups are delivered to us by Formspree, and our newsletter is sent through an email service provider that holds our mailing list on our behalf. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in any newsletter email, or by writing to us through our contact page.

Contributions

Contributions are processed by Stripe. When you contribute, you enter your payment details on Stripe's checkout page, not on our website. We never see or store your full card number. Stripe shares with us your name, email address, the amount and date of your contribution, and, for monthly contributions, the status of your subscription. We use this to keep records and to help you with questions about your contribution. Stripe's handling of your information is described in the Stripe Privacy Policy.

Information collected automatically

This website does not set advertising cookies and we do not build profiles of visitors.

We store one small note in your browser to remember that you have already seen our support message, so that it is not shown to you again for a week. It contains only a date and is not sent to us.

Like every website, the service that hosts ours receives basic technical information when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested. This is used to deliver the site and keep it secure.

Parts of our pages load from other services, which receive your IP address when they do:

  • Google Fonts supplies the typefaces.
  • Substack supplies the images on our Latest Articles page.
  • Shopify supplies the product images from our store.

If we add visitor analytics in the future, we will update this policy first.

Links to other services

Our site links to services we do not control, including our store, our bookstore, Substack, YouTube, X, Instagram, Facebook and Telegram. When you follow a link, subscribe or make a purchase there, that service's own privacy policy applies.

How we share information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share it only:

  • with service providers that help us run this website, deliver form submissions and process contributions, and only as needed for that work;
  • when the law requires it, or to protect our rights, our safety or the safety of others.

How long we keep information

We keep form submissions for as long as needed to respond and to keep a record of our correspondence. We keep contribution records for as long as needed for accounting and legal purposes.

Your choices

You can ask us what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. To do so, write to us through our contact page. We will respond within a reasonable time. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local law.

Children

This website is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information we hold. No method of sending or storing information online is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last changed.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through our contact page.
