Helpful resources.

Therapy First

Therapy First is a non-profit professional association of over 300 mental health providers worldwide who view psychotherapy as the appropriate first-line treatment for gender dysphoria.

Visit →

Gender Health Query

A resource and community for people concerned about same-sex attracted young people harmed by medical transition for gender dysphoria.

Visit →

GETA

The Gender Exploratory Therapy Association (GETA) provides access to therapists who will provide thoughtful care and tend to legitimate mental health concerns, without pushing an ideological or political agenda.

Visit →

PITT Parents

Parents with Inconvenient Truth about Trans (PITT) collects and recounts the personal thoughts, stories, and experiences of parents whose homes have been invaded by gender ideology.

Visit →

Rethink Identity Medicine Ethics

ReIME is a nonprofit education and research organization dedicated to improving ethical long term care and treatment for gender non-conforming children and youth.

Visit →

SEGM

Science For Evidence Based Gender Medicine (SEGM) is an international group of over 100 clinicians and researchers concerned about the lack of quality evidence for the use of hormonal and surgical interventions as first-line treatment for young people with gender dysphoria.

Visit →

Transition Justice

Transition Justice connects detransitioners and others negatively affected by gender medicine with legal assistance. If you have been harmed by transition-related interventions, they can set up a consultation to learn more about you and how we may be able to help.

Visit →

Affirming Reality

Affirming Reality is a program developed by Gabrielle Clark to help people parent their children into desistance. A step by step process designed to guide parents on how to engage their trans identifying kids.

Visit →

GCCAN

The Gender Care Consumer Advocacy Network (GCCAN) seeks to empower recipients of gender transition-related care to become healthy and whole.

Visit →

Transgender Trend

An organisation of parents, professionals and academics based in the UK who are concerned about the current trend to diagnose children as transgender, including the unprecedented number of teenage girls suddenly self-identifying as ‘trans’

Visit →

Child and Parental Rights Campaign

A nonprofit public-interest law firm founded to defend parents' rights to shield their children from the impacts of gender identity ideology.

Visit →

Gender Dysphoria Support Network

An international group that aims to offer psychoeducation and support to families of individuals affected by gender dysphoria.

Visit →

Parents of ROGD Kids

Parents of Rapid-Onset Gender Dysphoria Kids is a group of parents whose children have suddenly decided they identify strongly with the opposite sex and are at various stages in transitioning.

Visit →

Stats for Gender

Reliable data which is intuitively categorized and phrased in simple, jargon-free terms. Those who are questioning their gender should have full access to the facts.

Visit →

Genspect

An international alliance of professionals, parent groups, trans people, detransitioners, and others who seek high-quality care for gender distressed young people.

Visit →

Detrans Foundation

Detrans Foundation is a group of psychologists experienced in providing therapy to clients who are detransitioning or desisting and clients who experience regret about gender transition.

Visit →

4th Wave Now

A community of people who question the medicalization of gender-atypical youth.

Visit →

LGB Courage Coalition

A group of lesbian, gay and bisexual adults concerned about the state of gender medicine for children and teenagers in the United States and Canada.

Visit →

LGB Alliance

A UK charity that advocates for the rights and interests of lesbian, gay and bisexual people.

Visit →

Beyond Trans

Offers free therapeutic group programs for people experiencing gender-related distress, and support for families affected by it.

Visit →

Our Duty

A support network for parents with a child who believes they are transgender, active in the UK, USA, Australia and Canada.

Visit →

Bayswater Support Group

A UK support group for parents of trans-identified children and young people.

Visit →

Partners for Ethical Care

A parent-led organization that supports children and families struggling with gender identity and raises awareness of medicalization.

Visit →

Do No Harm

A group of medical professionals, students and policymakers working to keep ideology out of healthcare and to protect minors from gender medicine.

Visit →

The Cass Review

The final report of the UK's independent review of gender identity services for children and young people, published in April 2024.

Visit →

Defending Education

Works for an educational environment free from discrimination and political agendas, and helps parents challenge indoctrination in schools.

Visit →

Courage Is A Habit

Gives parents tools and tactics to protect their children and demand transparency from schools.

Visit →

Campbell Miller Payne

A nationwide law firm dedicated to representing detransitioners.
